When a compiled foreign fence captures build-time environment defaults but the corpus applies locale recipes only at execution, preserving discovery alone is insufficient. Bind a narrow explicit startup-key allowlist once into a cloned execution plan; distinguish absent from empty, retain embedded defaults when absent, and fingerprint the effective environment without rereading source files. Validate with deliberately different build/run values and native byte/status comparisons, including intentional error cases. Sprint562 PowerShell proof: e62d7996, 16 unchanged recipes, local native/interpreted/compiled gate.
